Thank you for contributing to DeerDawn. This agreement makes clear what rights
you grant when you contribute, so that the project, and everyone who uses it,
can rely on your contribution. It does not take your own rights away: you keep
the copyright in your contribution and can use it however you like.

## How to sign

The first time you open a pull request, a bot will ask you to sign. Sign by
posting this exact comment on the pull request:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

Your GitHub username, the pull request number and the time of signing are
recorded in the `cla-signatures` branch of this repository. You sign once; it
covers all your later contributions.

## Why a CLA and not a DCO

A Developer Certificate of Origin only certifies that you had the right to
submit your code under the project's current license. DeerDawn's server code
is under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0-only), and the
Project Owner also offers it under commercial licenses to organisations that
cannot accept the AGPL (dual licensing). A contribution received only under the
AGPL could not be included in those commercial licenses. That needs the
explicit relicensing grant in section 3, which a DCO does not give.
